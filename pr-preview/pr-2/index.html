What is LSSQ

Strangers become family.

LSSQ pops up somewhere new each month: a desert lot, a mountain forest, the Baja coast. Everyone brings their own gifts and talents to share, and the four pillars do the rest.

  • Wellness

    Morning yoga, sound baths and slow coffee.

  • Play

    Games, side quests and whatever someone dreams up.

  • Music

    Jams, DJ sets and songs around the fire.

  • Connection

    Strangers on Friday, family by Sunday.

Our journey

It started with one Saturday.

LSSQ began as single-day meetups on the last Saturday of the month. People kept asking for more, so it grew into weekend-long gatherings, shaped by the people who show up. The four pillars have stayed the same the whole way.
