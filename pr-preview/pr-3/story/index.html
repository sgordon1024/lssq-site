Our story

It started with one Saturday.

LSSQ began as single-day meetups on the last Saturday of the month, somewhere new each time. People kept asking for more, so it grew into weekend-long gatherings, shaped by the people who show up.

  1. 01

    One Saturday a month

    The idea was simple: pick a spot, roll in on the last Saturday of the month and meet your people. A new place every time, from desert lots to mountain forests to the Baja coast.

  2. 02

    Then whole weekends

    One day was never enough. The meetups grew into weekends, with time to settle in on Friday, be together all Saturday and pack up slowly on Sunday.

  3. 03

    Everyone brings something

    There's no lineup. People share their own gifts and talents: a yoga class, a jam, a meal for everyone. Wellness, play, music and connection hold it all together.

  4. 04

    Kept going by the family

    LSSQ runs almost entirely on donations. Every bit covers the land, the power and the little things that make a weekend feel like home.
