# How to update the LSSQ site

You don't need to know anything about websites. Tell an AI assistant what you want in plain words. It makes the change, shows you a preview, and publishes it when you say so.

## Once, with Steve

Steve will set this up with you. It takes about 15 minutes.

1. Make a free GitHub account, and Steve adds you to the site.
2. Pick an AI assistant that can work on GitHub projects, like Claude Code, ChatGPT's Codex or GitHub Copilot, and connect it to your GitHub account.
3. Point it at the project called **lssq-site**.

## Every time

1. **Say what you want**, the way you'd text a friend.
2. **Look at the preview.** The assistant sends you a link to the site with your change in it. Ask for fixes until it's right.
3. **Say "publish it."** The site updates about a minute later.

Nothing goes live until you say so. Change your mind later? Say "undo that."

## Things you can say

- "Add the March weekend: Joshua Tree, March 27 to 29. People can arrive from 2pm. Morning yoga, a sound bath and a potluck."
- "The spot for the March weekend is 34.1341, -116.3131. The last mile is sand, so take it slow."
- "Here's the WhatsApp link for the March weekend: ..."
- "Use this photo for the Baja weekend. Sam took it." (attach the photo)
- "Add these to the photos from past weekends." (attach the photos)
- "The April weekend is cancelled. Take it off the site."
- "Change the home page headline to ..."

## Photos

Attach them in the chat. Big photos straight from your phone are fine. If the assistant can't take them from the chat, it sends you a page where you drag them in and press **Commit changes**. Tell it who took each one, and only share photos you have the OK to share.

## Anything else

Design changes (colors, layout, new sections) go to Steve. If anything looks broken, tell Steve.
